About

Research-minded biotechnology graduate.

I am a biotechnology graduate from the International Islamic University Islamabad (IIUI), with a research focus on antimicrobial resistance and microbiology. My interest in this field grew out of an undergraduate thesis examining bacterial pathogens in hospital water systems — a project I carried out independently while most of my classmates worked in groups.

Alongside my research, I founded Biotech Squad, a student-led initiative that helps other biotechnology students access research training and opportunities. I am currently looking toward graduate research that builds on my wet-lab microbiology background and my interest in antimicrobial resistance.

Research Experience

Comparative Surveillance of ESKAPE Pathogens in Hospital Tap Water Systems

Undergraduate Research  /  AMR  /  Microbiology  /  Environmental Surveillance

For my BS thesis, I independently investigated hospital tap-water systems across six hospitals in Islamabad — three government and three private — for the presence of ESKAPE-associated bacterial pathogens and their resistance profiles. The project was supervised by Dr. Bushra Uzair, with research carried out in the Core Microbiology Laboratory at NUST’s Atta-ur-Rahman School of Applied Biosciences, under the co-supervision of Dr. Fazal Adnan.

Organisms investigated

  • Staphylococcus aureus — recovered from all six hospitals
  • Pseudomonas aeruginosa — recovered from three hospitals, confirmed by colony PCR targeting oprL (≈503 bp amplicon)
  • Klebsiella spp. — recovered from two hospitals

Methods

Samples were serially diluted in PBS (to 10-5) and plated across Mannitol Salt, Cetrimide, MacConkey, EMB, and Blood agar, with catalase testing and Kirby–Bauer disk diffusion on Mueller-Hinton agar used to assess antibiotic susceptibility. P. aeruginosa isolates were confirmed by colony PCR, and a 96-well crystal-violet biofilm assay was run in triplicate.

Setting3 government + 3 private hospitals
SupervisorDr. Bushra Uzair
Co-supervisorDr. Fazal Adnan
LaboratoryNUST Core Microbiology Laboratory

Research Workflow

How the thesis project was carried out.

  1. Hospital tap-water samples

    Collected from three government and three private hospitals.

  2. Sample processing

    Serial dilution in PBS, down to 10-5, with 20 µL plated per sample.

  3. Selective & differential culture

    Mannitol Salt, Cetrimide, MacConkey, EMB, and Blood agar.

  4. Biochemical identification

    Catalase testing to support isolate identification.

  5. Antibiotic susceptibility testing

    Kirby–Bauer disk diffusion on Mueller-Hinton agar.

  6. Colony PCR

    oprL target for P. aeruginosa, ≈503 bp amplicon.

  7. Biofilm assessment

    96-well crystal-violet assay, run in triplicate.

  8. Comparative analysis

    Government vs. private hospital isolates compared for resistance patterns.
